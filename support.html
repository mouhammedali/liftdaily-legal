Support

Most questions are answered below. For anything else, email mouhammedaliamer@gmail.com and expect a reply within a day or two.

Managing or canceling your subscription

Liftdaily Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are billed and managed entirely through Apple, not Liftdaily (there's no Liftdaily account to manage them from). To view, change, or cancel: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. You can also jump there from inside Liftdaily: Settings tab, then Manage Subscription.

Restoring a purchase

Switched to a new iPhone or reinstalled the app? Open Liftdaily's Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases. This checks your Apple ID's purchase history, since Liftdaily doesn't keep an account of its own.

Smart Import isn't working

Smart Import (pasting or photographing a plan) needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence available and turned on. If your device doesn't support it, PDF import and manual entry still work fully, no restrictions.

Is my data private?

Yes. Full details in the Privacy Policy, but in short: everything stays on your device, there are no Liftdaily servers, and nothing you enter is ever sent anywhere.

Exporting your data

Settings tab, then Export, gives you your full plan and logged history as a CSV or a JSON backup.
