Liftdaily

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version: Liftdaily has no user accounts. Your workout plans, logs, and progress stay on your device (and, if you use it, are shared directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch). The app sends anonymous usage events, such as "a plan was added" or "a workout was finished", so we can see where people get stuck. They never include your plan, lifts, or notes, and you can turn them off in Settings. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to track you across other apps or websites.

What Liftdaily accesses, and why

  • Apple Health. With your permission, Liftdaily reads your heart rate and active energy during a live workout to show metrics on Apple Watch, and writes your completed strength workouts to Health so they count toward your activity rings. If you turn on "Save workouts to Health" in the iPhone app's Settings, workouts you finish on your iPhone without Apple Watch are written to Health too. This data is read from and written to your own Health data store on your device. Liftdaily does not have its own copy on any server, because Liftdaily has no servers.
  • Photos. If you use Smart Import's photo option, Liftdaily uses Apple's system photo picker, which only gives the app the one photo you actively select. Liftdaily never gets access to your full photo library.
  • Your workout plans and logs. Everything you build or import (training days, exercises, logged sets, weights, reps, and progress) is stored locally in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Apple Watch sync. If you use the Apple Watch app, your plan and logs are transferred directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework: a direct device-to-device channel, not a Liftdaily server.

Smart Import and on-device AI

Smart Import turns a pasted message or a photo of a plan into a structured workout using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework and on-device text recognition (Vision). This processing happens entirely on your iPhone. The text or photo you provide is never sent to Liftdaily, Apple, or anyone else over the network for this feature.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn which steps people reach and where they give up, Liftdaily and its App Clip send anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, on its EU servers. An event says what happened and a few counts or choices, for example "a plan was imported, from a pasted message, with 3 days", "a starter was chosen: wolf", "a set was logged", or "the Pro screen was shown". PostHog also records basic technical details: the app version, the device model, the iOS version, the language and the time zone.

  • Events are tied to a random ID created when the app is installed, not to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other account. They are not linked to your identity.
  • They never contain the text of your plans, exercise names, weights, reps, notes, photos, or any Health data.
  • No location is looked up from your IP address, and IP addresses are not stored.
  • The data is used only to improve Liftdaily. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can turn it off at any time in the app: Settings, "Share anonymous usage". Nothing more is sent once it is off.

What Liftdaily does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile of any kind.
  • No advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No Liftdaily servers for your training: your plans, logs, and progress are never uploaded. The only thing the app sends over the network is the anonymous usage events described above.

Purchases

Liftdaily Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Liftdaily never sees or stores your payment details. Refunds, billing, and subscription management all go through your Apple ID, in Settings or the App Store.

Data deletion

Since your training data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes your Liftdaily data with it, and a new install gets a new random analytics ID. Because usage events are not linked to you, we can't look them up by name or email; they are kept for at most 12 months. Health data you've written stays under Apple Health's own controls (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health), independent of Liftdaily.

Children's privacy

Liftdaily does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous usage events described above, which identify no one.
